  <div class="text-foreground/70 leading-relaxed">
    <p class="text-lg mb-6">
      Parents aren't asking for a better search engine. They're asking for help at 8:14pm, when their child is staring at a textbook problem neither of them can crack, and the YouTube autoplay has cycled through four explanations that don't match. That is the moment Gurukool is designed for. The moment a parent needs an answer, an explanation, and the reassurance that someone else has been there.

    </p>
