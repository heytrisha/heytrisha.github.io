{/* The Problem */}

<section class="mb-20">
  <span class="text-sm font-medium tracking-wider uppercase text-violet-400">The Problem</span>
  <h2 class="text-3xl md:text-4xl font-bold mt-4 mb-8">Event discovery is broken, for both sides.</h2>

<p class="mb-8 text-lg leading-relaxed text-gray-400">
  Event platforms today feel like endless catalogues. For Explorers, it's decision fatigue: hundreds
  of listings, no personalization, and no way to interact with hosts before committing. For
  Creators, it's invisibility: even great events struggle to be seen, forcing them to promote on
  social media instead of using built-in tools.
</p>

  <div class="grid md:grid-cols-2 gap-6 mb-8">
    <div class="border border-white/10 rounded-xl p-6 bg-white/[0.02]">
      <h3 class="text-lg font-semibold text-[#fafafa] mb-2">Listings, zero relevance</h3>
      <p class="text-gray-400 text-sm leading-relaxed">Most platforms surface every event equally, leaving users to do the filtering themselves.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="border border-white/10 rounded-xl p-6 bg-white/[0.02]">
      <h3 class="text-lg font-semibold text-[#fafafa] mb-2">Filters, still no match</h3>
      <p class="text-gray-400 text-sm leading-relaxed">Date, location, price. Niche interests are still buried under generic categories.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="border border-white/10 rounded-xl p-6 bg-white/[0.02]">
      <h3 class="text-lg font-semibold text-[#fafafa] mb-2">Abandoned searches</h3>
      <p class="text-gray-400 text-sm leading-relaxed">Users drop off when overwhelmed by options and lack of guidance.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="border border-white/10 rounded-xl p-6 bg-white/[0.02]">
      <h3 class="text-lg font-semibold text-[#fafafa] mb-2">Promotion scattered across channels</h3>
      <p class="text-gray-400 text-sm leading-relaxed">Hosts juggle Instagram, email, and DMs because the platform gives them no built-in reach.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>
