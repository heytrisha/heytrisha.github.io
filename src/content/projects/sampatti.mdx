{/* The Solution */}

<section class="mb-20">
  <span class="text-sm font-medium tracking-wider uppercase text-amber-400">The Solution</span>
  <h2 class="text-3xl md:text-4xl font-bold mt-4 mb-8">Sampatti is built for the user who is still deciding.</h2>

  <div class="mb-8">
    <p class="text-foreground/70 leading-relaxed">Sampatti replaces the overwhelming fund catalogue with a single personalised recommendation based on two questions about your financial situation and risk tolerance. The first SIP starts at ₹100, pause anytime, stop anytime, no exit fees. Every term is explained in plain language where it appears, not buried in a glossary. Projections are calculated from your actual income and age, not generic calculators. And the guidance adapts as you invest, growing with you from first SIP to multiple goals.</p>
  </div>
</section>
