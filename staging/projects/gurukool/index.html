Gurukool

Designing a parent-focused education app with guided homework help, a trusted community, and an AI assistant. A 2025 case study.

Education Mobile App Community UX Design

“How might we help parents support their child’s learning journey in a guided, stress-free way?”

  • Guided Homework Support

    Step-by-step assistance so parents never feel stuck explaining a concept.

  • Trusted Parent Community

    A space for parents to connect, share tips, and learn from others going through the same journey.

  • AI-Powered Assistant

    Instant, personalized help via homework scans, voice notes, and contextual explanations.

The Problem

What parents told us

Parents want to help. But the way children learn today is different from how they were taught. They feel stuck, uncertain, and alone.

Concepts have changed

”I don’t always remember the concepts, and sometimes I feel like I don’t even know the right way to explain them.”

Teaching methods differ

”The way kids are taught today is so different from how I learned. I’m not sure if I’m guiding them the right way.”

Need instant help

”When my child asks a question, I need help instantly. I can’t spend hours searching for trustworthy explanations online.”

Seeking community

”Sometimes I just want to know how other parents are handling this, it would feel reassuring to share tips and struggles.”

The Solution

Guided help, AI support, and a community, in one place

Gurukool replaces the chaos of searching online with a structured, guided experience. Parents start at Home, where they see recent activity, community updates, and quick access to their most-used tools. When homework gets tough, Guided Help offers topic videos, practice sheets, and step-by-step explanations. The AI assistant answers instantly through scans or voice notes. No more searching for trustworthy sources. And the Community connects parents who share the same struggles, so no one feels alone.

Structured over scattered

Everything organized into Home, Guided Help, Community, and Tool Kit.

Guided over guessing

Step-by-step explanations so parents never feel stuck.

Instant over searching

AI answers in seconds via scans or voice.

Community over isolation

Parents connect and learn from others facing the same challenges.

The thinking

Help at 8:14pm.

Parents aren’t asking for a better search engine. They’re asking for help at 8:14pm, when their child is staring at a textbook problem neither of them can crack, and the YouTube autoplay has cycled through four explanations that don’t match. That is the moment Gurukool is designed for. The moment a parent needs an answer, an explanation, and the reassurance that someone else has been there.

The product replaces scattered searching with a single trustworthy space: a home, a guided help layer, a community of parents in the same boat, and a tool kit for the everyday friction. The bet is that structured guidance outperforms infinite options for parents who already feel overwhelmed.
